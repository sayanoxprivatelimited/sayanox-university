Privacy

No account or analytics service is required by this site. Notes, code drafts, progress, quiz scores, study activity and goals, study time and certificate records are stored locally in your browser, if storage is available.

Your exported backup is unencrypted and can contain personal information and code. Keep it safe. Import replaces local learning data after confirmation. Clearing browser data removes local records; use your browser settings to delete them.

The site itself does not send your learning data to a server. Your hosting provider may process ordinary web requests and server logs. External links have their own privacy policies.
